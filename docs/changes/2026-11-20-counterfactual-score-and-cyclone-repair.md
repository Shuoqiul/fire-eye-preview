# Counterfactual score, footprint and cyclone repair

The counterfactual map now shows an explicitly labelled **illustrative bounded published-score scenario**. Baseline stays on the released occupancy grid and the original map rendering. Scenario values are computed from the same published cell through a paired native-ISI odds shift, `d=clamp(0.25 log((1+ISI_scenario)/(1+ISI_baseline)), -1, 1)` and `q=s exp(d)/(1-s+s exp(d))`, which preserves exact zero and one values. Zero shift leaves the original cell and color in place. Scenario coloring copies the app's `0`, `.03`, `.05`, `.08`, `.15`, `.25`, `.4`, `.6`, and `1` color knots. Change uses a separate maximum absolute cell-score difference; raw readout shows published score, scenario score, change, and native ISI as separate units. Stored score deltas are recomputed from the two Float32 values used for display and readout.

FFMC and ISI remain native mechanism diagnostics. Without a model JVP or learned paired rerun, the `0.25` coupling remains a documented illustrative coefficient from the project's public prototype assumptions. It supplies a bounded scenario score rather than a predicted probability. Dry thunder can open the comparison even without added rain; its current ISI and map score remain exactly unchanged, while discovery/holdover and exposure-conditioned ignition stay separate.

The tropical-cyclone engine uses North Atlantic Knaff R34/R64 wind geometry and local 1-minute wind as ISI forcing. R-CLIPER rain has one finite radial support, `Rmax + Re log(Tmax / 0.05)`, and the rate reaches exactly zero at that cutoff. It tapers linearly from the maximum-rate radius to zero at the cutoff. The same support and taper drive footprint selection and native rain forcing. The deprecated 300 km convective Gaussian is removed. Below 34 kt, a coherent radial core is scaled down toward zero wind; a zero-wind or post-track storm has no selected support. Native loop wind is explicitly converted once from knots to m/s. Postlandfall profiles retain the existing weak-wind lower bound and a continuous coastline-dependent taper; this work adds no calibration.

Checks cover heterogeneous published bins, exact zero and NaN preservation, dry and wet thunder, distant and postcoast cyclones, and high-wind Gaussian clipping. Browser checks cover real `gridFor` values, baseline opacity/layer preservation, dry Change with exact published values, wet score change, and the available local app scenario. The local sandbox blocks Chromium startup and loopback server binding; the host acceptance environment runs the browser scripts.
